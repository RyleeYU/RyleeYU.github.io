Sketches as Previews: How Humans Sketch Visual Representations for Videos

A formative study of how people sketch, evaluate and revise static visual representations of dynamic video content.

Overview

This empirical formative study investigates how people translate dynamic video content into static sketches. It does not claim a deployed AI preview system; instead, it builds evidence for how future human- and AI-in-the-loop preview tools might work.

Approach & Methods

The team designed a sketch–evaluate–revise workflow and conducted a study with 42 participants across 14 three-person sessions, examining visual encoding strategies and peer-driven revisions.

My Contributions

Rui co-developed the research concept, questions and analytical framework, and co-designed the study workflow.

Findings & Implications

The study surfaced trade-offs among meaning, distinctiveness and drawability, alongside persistent gaps in representing camera motion. Participant sketches are not displayed here pending anonymization and publication review.
