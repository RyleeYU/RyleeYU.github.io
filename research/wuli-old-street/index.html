Research on College Students' Rural Practice Activities: Historical Analysis and Development Path Exploration of Wuli Old Street under the Maritime Silk Road Culture

An undergraduate fieldwork project on cultural heritage communication and local development around Wuli Old Street.

Project overview board combining fieldwork and archival imagery from Wuli Old Street.

Overview

This undergraduate project examined cultural heritage communication and development around Wuli Old Street in the context of Maritime Silk Road culture.

Approach & Methods

The five-person team combined literature review, fieldwork and archival research with interviews involving government officials, residents, tourists and local stakeholders.

My Contributions

As team leader, Rui coordinated the research and synthesized interview findings, policy documents and field observations into a final report.
