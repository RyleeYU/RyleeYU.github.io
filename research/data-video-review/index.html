Understanding Human-AI Collaboration in Data Video Creation: A Review of Academic Prototypes and Commercial Tools

A structured review of 19 academic prototypes and 22 commercial tools through a six-stage workflow and four-dimensional analytical lens.

Overview

This review asks how human–AI collaboration is supported across the process of creating data videos. It compares 19 academic prototypes and 22 commercial tools through a six-stage workflow lens and four analytical dimensions.

Approach & Methods

The study combines structured literature screening, binary capability coding, hands-on evaluation of 22 commercial tools with a 0–4 rubric, and statistical analysis. It produced a manuscript, tool database and supplementary research site.

My Contributions

As team leader and first author, Rui developed the analytical framework and led the screening, coding, tool evaluation and analysis process.

Findings & Implications

The review identifies fragmented workflow support, uneven human control and limitations in explicit data grounding. These gaps inform design implications for more coherent and accountable creative tools.
