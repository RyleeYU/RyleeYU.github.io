Rui YU

Hi! I'm Rui YU , a second-year MPhil student in the Computational Media and Arts Thrust at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) . I'm fortunate to be supervised by Prof. Pan Hui↗ and co-supervised by Prof. Anyi Rao↗.

My main research interests lie in HCI for Creativity and Social Computing. I'm also interested in Human–Robot Interaction and Digital Health, and I've had opportunities to explore these areas through different research projects. I enjoy exploring how people interact with emerging technologies and how we can design more meaningful experiences.

Outside of research, I love experiences that allow me to feel completely immersed in the moment—whether it's swimming 🏊, getting lost in a good book 📖, watching a film 🎬 from beginning to end, or practicing Chinese calligraphy. I also have an adorable turquoise-Isabel lovebird 🦜 who brings a lot of joy to my everyday life!

I'm currently looking for PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027 or Spring 2028. I'm always happy to connect, exchange ideas, or explore potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out! 📬
